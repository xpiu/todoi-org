==========================

own site on 2026-09-17, resized to 48x48 and saved as lossless WebP. Nothing is
redrawn. Trademarks belong to their respective projects and are used here to
identify them.

Notes
-----
nextcloud-deck  The linked project is Nextcloud Deck, an app inside Nextcloud;
                the mark is nextcloud.com's, matching the URL the row links to.
                It is the only source published at 32x32, so it is the softest
                of the eleven at 2x.
openproject     Their apple-touch-icon is a small mark on a large white
                field. The field is flood-filled to transparency and the mark
                scaled to the same optical weight as the rest, so it does not
                show as a white tile on zebra, hovered or expanded rows.

kanboard        Both publish a dark glyph on an opaque white tile. The tile
worklenz        is flood-filled to transparency here and redrawn by style.css
                on the dark theme only, so it cannot appear and disappear as
                zebra parity changes under a sort.
